The Association Rules
1

Show Up

RSVP yes and actually appear. Flaking without notice is a violation of association code.

2

Yap Freely

No topic is off limits on the yap board. Oversharing is encouraged. Undersharing is suspicious.

3

Propose Boldly

Have an idea? Submit it. Bad ideas welcome. The association votes; the group decides.

4

Dress for the Vibe

Check the event theme. There's no such thing as overdressed for a MADDY event.

5

Vote on the Polls

Democracy is the backbone of this association. Every voice counts, especially yours.
